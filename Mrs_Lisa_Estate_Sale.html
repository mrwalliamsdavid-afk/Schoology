Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked

Why are the prices thoughtfully reduced? +
The estate is being settled during relocation. Rather than paying auction commissions or long storage, these select items are offered at reduced prices directly to school community families.
Are hold deposits fully refundable? +
Yes, 100% fully refundable. All reservation funds are held in trust by The Property Law Firm CB LLP and returned immediately if an item does not meet your inspection standards.
Why must I write from my personal email? +
School and employer servers use strict spam firewalls that often quarantine messages from legal liaisons. A personal address (Gmail, Yahoo, iCloud, Outlook) ensures you receive prompt replies.
What details should I provide in my inquiry? +
Please provide: your full name, home address, phone number, personal email address, and the school where you found the announcement.
