Questions

Frequently asked

Quick answers to the questions families ask most.

Why should I reply from my personal email?+
School and work email filters often reject messages from outside addresses. Using a personal account (such as Gmail or iCloud) helps make sure your message reaches Cheryl.
What information should I include in my message?+
Please include your name, your phone number, your address, the school where you found the listings, and the exact item you are interested in.
Why do you need my phone number?+
So Cheryl can reach you quickly about the item you asked about.
What does “Sold” mean?+
A sold item is no longer available. Sold items remain listed so you can see what has already been claimed.
Are photos and vehicle details available?+
Many items have photos on this page. For items without photos, photos and vehicle details are available on request. Ask Cheryl through the chat or the contact form.
Can I share this listing?+
Absolutely. Please share it with anyone who may be interested. Thank you for your kindness.
